ILMORA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

ILMORA ("we", "our") is built by madrasa students to help people pray, read Quran and support madrasa education. This policy explains what we collect and why — in plain language.

1. What we collect

  • Account (optional): if you create an account, we store your name and email (Firebase Authentication).
  • Orders & donations: name, mobile number, product/donation amount, payment reference (UTR) and order status — used only to verify your payment and deliver your purchase.
  • Device identifier: a random ID used to prevent fraud (blocked users, download limits).
  • Push token: if you enable notifications, a token is stored so we can send you announcements.
  • Location: used only while the app is open, only for Qibla direction and prayer times. We do not store your location.

2. What we NEVER do

  • We never sell or rent your data.
  • We never show your details to other users.
  • We never store your card/bank passwords. Payments happen through UPI directly from your own payment app.

3. Data storage

Data is stored securely on Google Firebase (ilmora-app-f84e8). Access is restricted: only you can read your own account/orders, and only authorised admins can view order records for verification.

4. Deletion

You can request deletion of your account or orders any time by contacting us in the app (Settings → Talk to support).

5. Children

ILMORA is safe for all ages and does not collect data from children beyond what is described above for orders made by their guardians.
